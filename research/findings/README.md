# Findings

One short write-up per research question, named after it (`rq1-lifecycle.md`, `rq2-lead-lag.md` and so on). Each says:

- the question;
- the data used: its dates, `algo_version` and row counts;
- the method;
- the result, with the key numbers;
- what it means for the forecasts;
- its limits.

The key numbers also go into PLAN.md › Predictions and research.
